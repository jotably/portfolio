Waste management system

Detect when a public trash can is full, to help with overflowing bins and litter in dense cities.

My role
Team project.
Context
Team project.
Category
Physical computing

Process

  • Measure from the lid. A distance sensor inside the lid looks down at the trash. As the can fills, the distance shrinks, so one sensor and one threshold answer the question without weighing anything.
  • Clear output. Below the threshold the LED turns on and the serial output reads "Trash Full." Otherwise it reads "Trash OK."
  • Two-part enclosure. One part on the lid holds the Arduino, breadboard and LED. The other fixes the sensor inside the lid.
  • Simulate first. We drafted and tested the circuit and code in TinkerCAD before the physical build.

Results

  • Tested in three trials at rising fill levels, against a requirement of a response within 2 to 3 seconds.
