Met residential and art tower

Turn an underused storage facility into a mixed-use development for the Metropolitan Museum of Art: art storage, education space and a residential tower.

My role
I designed the residential floor plans and unit layouts.
Context
ACE Mentor Program of Greater New York, Team 42 at Beyer Blinder Belle.
Category
CAD and design

Process

  • Unit mix. Smaller one-bedroom units raise the number of affordable homes inside a fixed footprint, and two-bedroom units of up to 1,000 sq ft keep the building usable for families.
  • Balance. Unit density, hallway and elevator circulation, and livability all had to work under New York City building codes.

Results

  • 250 apartments within the tower footprint.
  • 29% affordable housing.
