Radar detection

Sweep an ultrasonic range sensor through 180° on a servo and report the angle and distance of any object within 50 cm.

My role
I designed the mount that holds the sensor on the servo on my own, and built the circuit and wrote the code with my group.
Context
Group project.
Category
Physical computing

Process

  • First prototype. The mount attached only to the servo horn and was unstable.
  • Second and third. Legs on the base fixed the wobble but blocked the servo's rotation.
  • Final. The support stays clear of the sweep.
  • Measuring distance. The sensor sends a pulse of sound and times the echo. Sound travels about 0.034 cm per microsecond and the pulse goes out and back, so distance = echo time × 0.034 ÷ 2.

Results

  • A stable mount that sweeps the full 180° without obstruction.
  • Each reading pairs a servo angle with a distance, which gives the object's position.
